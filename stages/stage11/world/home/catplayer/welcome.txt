Ten stages of commands. Now the question changes.

Not "what do I type" but "what actually happened when I typed it".

When you run ls, six things happen before a single filename appears.
The shell forks. The copy replaces itself with the ls program. ls asks
the kernel to open a directory. The kernel checks whether you are
allowed. It reads the disk. The answer comes back.

You are going to watch each of those steps for real.
