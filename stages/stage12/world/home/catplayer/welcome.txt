Stage 12, {{PLAYER}}.

Stage 4 taught you that ps lists processes and kill stops them. That was
the tourist version.

Every process has a parent. Every process is in a state. Every kill sends
a specific signal, and the process usually gets to decide what to do
about it - except for one signal, which it cannot refuse.

By the end of this stage you will make a zombie on purpose, and know
exactly whose fault it is.
