Everything so far happened one thing at a time. Real systems do not.

When two threads share a variable, the obvious code is wrong - and it is
wrong intermittently, which is far worse than being wrong always. It will
pass your tests and fail in production.

There are four demos here. Run them. Concurrency is the one topic where
reading the explanation genuinely does not work; you need to watch a
counter come out wrong.
