Stage 14, {{PLAYER}}.

Your machine has a handful of cores and hundreds of processes. They are
not all running. Most are asleep, and the few that want the CPU take
turns so fast it looks simultaneous.

The scheduler decides the order. Which order it picks changes how long
each job waits - and the same set of jobs can finish in wildly different
average times depending only on that choice.

The simulator here lets you try each algorithm on the same jobs and
compare the numbers.
