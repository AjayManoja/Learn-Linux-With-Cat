Stage 7, {{PLAYER}}.

You have been finding files by name. Names are the least interesting
thing about a file.

Here you will find them by what they are: a directory or a file, big or
small, changed today or untouched for months. Then you will do something
to every one you found, without typing their names.

And you will learn where your space has gone, which is the question
every full disk eventually forces you to ask.
